# 09 — 2018 World Models: V, M, C

The [Ha & Schmidhuber World Models paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.10122) provides a great conceptual bridge: learn a compressed view of an environment and a dynamics model, then train a simple controller.

## Vision (V): VAE

A convolutional VAE maps image `o_t` to a latent distribution `q(z_t|o_t)` and samples a compact `z_t`. Decoder reconstructs images. This stage learns spatial compression independently from control. Because the objective is reconstruction, visually salient details can be retained even if not reward-relevant.

## Memory (M): mixture-density RNN

An RNN receives current latent and action and predicts parameters for a **mixture of Gaussians** over the next latent state:

\[p(z_{t+1}\mid z_{\le t},a_{\le t})=\sum_{k=1}^K\pi_k\,\mathcal N(z_{t+1};\mu_k,\Sigma_k).\]

Here `π_k` are mixture weights that sum to one, `μ_k` component means, and `Σ_k` often diagonal covariances. A mixture represents multiple qualitatively plausible successors; a single MSE predictor averages them.

A controller may use `z_t` and RNN hidden state `h_t` to select action. The M module can generate simulated future latent sequences, sometimes decoded to images.

## Controller (C)

A deliberately small policy is trained using learned features. In the original work, controller optimization used evolution strategies rather than requiring gradient backpropagation through a giant policy. Some experiments trained a controller inside dream rollouts generated by M and transferred it to the environment.

## Why it mattered

- Showed that **representation + transition + controller** could be separated.
- Introduced the intuition of **learning in imagination**.
- Highlighted model exploitation: an agent may find bugs in its invented dream and perform poorly in reality.

## What it did not solve

There was no guarantee learned latents corresponded to physical state; dynamics errors compound; training the components independently can lose important information; visually good reconstructions don't imply good controllability. Subsequent PlaNet/Dreamer systems learned richer recurrent latent models using jointly trained reconstruction/dynamics objectives.

## Compare with an RSSM

`V` corresponds broadly to an observation encoder; `M` is a stochastic RNN dynamics model; `C` is policy. Modern RSSM instead typically couples recurrence, probabilistic latent state, prior/posterior inference, reward prediction, and representation training within one sequence model.
